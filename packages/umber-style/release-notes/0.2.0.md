## umber-style@0.2.0

A release for the new ranges of its dependencies, with nothing changed in this package.
([#52](https://github.com/soroush-tech/umber-style/issues/52))

### Changed

- Through the core and the bindings at 0.2.0: `umber-style/react` forwards from a string tag the props that `@umber-style/is-prop-valid/react` is true for, a wider list than before, and two copies of the core or of a binding in one program share what one registers and another reads.
  ([#56](https://github.com/soroush-tech/umber-style/issues/56))
  [docs](https://github.com/soroush-tech/umber-style/blob/main/docs/how-it-works.md#two-copies-in-one-program)
- The prop filters are not re-exported here: import `@umber-style/is-prop-valid/react`, `/vue` or `/react-native`.
  [docs](https://github.com/soroush-tech/umber-style/blob/main/docs/prop-filters.md)

### Packaging

- Depends on `@umber-style/core`, `@umber-style/react`, `@umber-style/react-native` and `@umber-style/vue` `^0.2.0` (each was the exact `0.1.0`): they are declared `workspace:^`. A patch of one of them now reaches this package without a release of its own, and an app keeps one copy.
- MINOR, with the core and the bindings: new public API and a wider default filter.
- Peer dependencies: `react` `^19.2.8` and `vue` `^3.5.0`, unchanged.
